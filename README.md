Why Ricardo De Melo Matos
I’m Ricardo de Melo Matos, a communications and marketing professional with a background that connects storytelling, technology, business development, and digital strategy. My career has taken me across journalism, broadcasting, marketing, public relations, digital products, and business development, giving me a multidisciplinary perspective on how ideas become meaningful experiences and, ultimately, business opportunities. I enjoy working at the intersection of creativity and strategy—understanding a problem, researching the market, finding the story, and building a solution that connects with people.

This repository is a small introduction to how I think, work, and approach challenges. Today, through my work with Blü AI Technologies in the United States, I contribute to marketing and business development strategies for Canadian digital products such as DynaCore, OfferioAI, Milo, and NeuroAI, helping position technology for the Canadian and U.S. markets. I created “Why Ricardo de Melo Matos” because I believe a professional profile should be more than a résumé—it should be a reflection of curiosity, continuous learning, experimentation, and the willingness to build something new. This GitHub is part portfolio, part laboratory, and part record of that journey.
